## What to analyze
1. Identify the video with the highest click-through rate.
2. Identify the video with the strongest average view duration.
3. Compare thumbnail performance and title patterns.
4. Note the first major retention drop in each long-form video.
5. Identify which Short generated the most profile/channel visits.
6. Record the top audience questions and turn them into future topics.

## Week 2 actions
- Repeat topics with strong retention.
- Test a new thumbnail/title combination where impressions are high but CTR is weak.
- Turn frequently asked questions into Shorts.
- Improve the first 15–30 seconds of videos with early drop-off.
- Maintain a simple weekly analytics log.
